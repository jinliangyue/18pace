Real experience

The 15K wall.

The mistake that cost me 25 seconds per kilometer.

5:10 → 5:35

Skipped the second gel.

At my sub 1:50 attempt, I skipped my second gel because I "felt fine". By km 16, my pace dropped from 5:10 to 5:35. I never recovered.

25g

Pre-emptive, not reactive.

Don't wait until you're hungry or tired. By the time you feel it, your glycogen is already depleted. Take gels before you need them.

60-80g

Total carbs for 1:50 effort.

Your body needs roughly 60-80g of carbs during a 1:50 effort. Two well-timed gels (25g each) at km 8-9 and km 14-15 covers this without overwhelming your stomach.
