Real experience

Why treadmills feel different.

Setting the belt to 11.5 km/h felt easier than the same pace on asphalt.

Air

Air resistance factor.

Outdoors, your body expends energy pushing through air. On a treadmill, you stay stationary in a climate-controlled room. To replicate the outdoor load, set the incline to at least 1.0%.

Belt

Belt assistance.

The motorized belt actively pulls your leg backward. If you do more than 30% of your 12-week training on a flat treadmill, your hamstrings will be undertrained for real road impact on race day.
