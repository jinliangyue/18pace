The plan

The race has three parts.

A sub-1:50 isn't run at one pace. It's run in three.

01
0 – 10 KM

Hold back.

Your biggest mistake is going out too fast. 5:20–5:30/km feels embarrassingly slow at the start. That's intentional.

Target 5:20–5:30 / km
02
10 – 18 KM

Settle in.

This is where patience pays. Stay close to your target pace. Save energy for the wall at 17.

Target 5:13 / km
03
18 – 21.1 KM

Now race.

If you still have something left at 18, this is where you use it. The clock rewards every second under 1:50.

Hold 5:13 · Finish strong

Real experience

What 1:50 actually feels like.

Advice from someone who has actually run it.

10 KM

Holding back is hard.

At the start, 5:20–5:30/km can feel frustratingly slow. That's intentional. The first 10K is a controlled warmup disguised as a race.

15 – 18 KM

The wall is real.

Your job here isn't to get faster. It's to keep moving efficiently. Gel timing matters here. Skip it and you'll pay.

18 – 21.1 KM

Now you race.

If you've done everything right, the final 3K is where you earn the finish. Hold your pace through 20. Sprint the last 1.1 if you can.
